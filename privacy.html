Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Turnwood is a wood-turning game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The game has no sign-in, shows no ads, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs and does not ask for tracking permission.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Career progress and settings are saved only in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Purchases go through Apple. The one-time Full Version purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, and the game sends no receipt or identifier anywhere.
  • No network use. The game itself does not connect to the internet. The only traffic is the App Store's own purchase check.
  • Children. The game is for a general audience and collects no data from anyone, including children.

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released.
