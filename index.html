Turnwood

A realistic wood-turning game for iPhone and iPad. Turn blanks on the lathe, fill orders, and keep your best pieces on the shelf.

Support

For a problem, a question or feedback, write to lkmcelik@hotmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I restore my Full Version purchase?
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore next to Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?
Yes. Progress is saved only on your device.
The game stops my music.
It should not: Turnwood plays its sounds alongside other apps. If it happens, please write to us.

Sık sorulanlar

Oyunda Ayarlar'ı açın ve Satın alımlar satırındaki Geri yükle'ye dokunun. Satın aldığınız Apple Hesabı'nı kullanın.
Oyunu silersem ilerlemem kaybolur mu?
